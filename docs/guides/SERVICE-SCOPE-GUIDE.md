# Freelance service scope guide

Use this to explain the difference between a presentation website and a transactional business platform before quoting a client.

## 1. Small-business / landing website

Typical scope:
- Responsive homepage plus agreed supporting pages.
- About, services/products, contact and location sections.
- WhatsApp, phone, email and social links.
- Basic metadata and accessible responsive components.

Not automatically included: CMS, customer accounts, payment processing, inventory, booking engine or custom backend.

## 2. Restaurant / cafe website

Typical scope:
- Restaurant/cafe homepage and story.
- Menu categories, item descriptions and prices.
- Opening hours, address/map link and contact CTA.
- Optional WhatsApp/call-to-order entry point.

Not automatically included: table management, live reservations, kitchen display, POS integration, delivery dispatch or payment collection.

## 3. Product catalogue / ecommerce front end

Typical scope:
- Product and category browsing.
- Product cards, price display and responsive layouts.
- Frontend cart prototype or catalog inquiry flow.
- Shipping/returns information pages.

A real ecommerce checkout needs a separately scoped backend or commerce provider, inventory and order rules, payment integration, customer notifications and security testing.

## 4. Food ordering / delivery front end

Typical scope:
- Menu listing and category navigation.
- Item description, options and price presentation.
- Cart prototype and clear order CTA.
- Delivery/pickup information.

A production ordering system needs server-side order creation, order status, payment confirmation, delivery zone/fee logic, staff workflow and customer notifications.

## Quote and change control

Before quoting, confirm page count, content readiness, custom design needs, integrations, hosting, deadline, revision rounds and post-launch support. Treat new integrations and new page types as scope changes rather than silently absorbing them.
