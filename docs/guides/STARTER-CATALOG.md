## Shared system

The complete starter system also includes:

- Reusable UI: buttons, badges, cards, breadcrumbs, category pills, price display, forms, notices, promo banners, FAQs, business hours, image captions, product cards and menu items.
- Food ordering experience: product categories, size variants, add-ons, cart quantities, item removal, pickup/delivery choice, delivery fee calculation and WhatsApp order handoff.
- Client website generator: brand configuration, logo/photo paths, colors, typography, page section toggles, component layout variants, product/menu data and a separate client project folder.
- QA and delivery documentation: integration plan, privacy/security reminders, accessibility and responsiveness checklist, launch checklist and client setup instructions.

## Important demo boundaries

The included carts and wishlists are browser-local demonstrations. WhatsApp order handoff prepares a message for the customer to review and send. It does not store orders on a server, charge a payment method, confirm inventory or arrange a courier. Real production flows need the client's selected provider, server-side integration and end-to-end verification.

Replace demo product copy, prices, remote photos, contact details, addresses, policies and testimonials with client-approved content before launch.

## Furniture & home store

Route: `/templates/furniture`

A calm, editorial furniture storefront inspired by the supplied Beetle Pro reference: neutral materials, a chair-led hero, shop-by-room categories, featured products, room inspiration, brand story and showroom contact. The product area includes category filters and a local enquiry bag that prepares a WhatsApp enquiry; it is not a payment checkout or stock system. Replace the sample brand, ETB prices, imagery, WhatsApp number, showroom address and opening hours before launch.
